# Work Eligibility and Application Details

This section answers the standard logistics and self-identification questions asked in application questionnaires (Workday, Greenhouse, Lever, and similar systems). This information is only used for job-fit and application-screening analysis, not for open-ended conversation.

**Location and relocation:** I am open to relocating for the right role; location alone is not a blocker.

**Work arrangement:** I am willing and able to work onsite or hybrid at an office in San Francisco or New York City, or any other location a role requires. I can accommodate any hybrid schedule with a specific, fixed number of required in-office days per week (2, 3, 4, or fully onsite) that an employer sets — I have no preference for remote over hybrid/onsite or vice versa, and no scheduling constraint that would prevent meeting a specific in-office day requirement. Any of these arrangements is a fit, not a gap or partial match.

**Work authorization:** I am currently authorized to work in the United States on F-1 OPT (Optional Practical Training). I will require employer sponsorship for a work visa (such as H-1B) in the future to continue working in the US beyond OPT.

**Race/ethnicity:** Asian / South Asian.

**Gender:** Man.

**Veteran status:** Not a veteran.

**Disability status:** Not disabled; no disability.

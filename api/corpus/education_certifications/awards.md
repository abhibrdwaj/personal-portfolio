# Hackathons & Professional Awards

I have a strong track record of competitive innovation, most recently demonstrated by winning the Rutgers Health Hackathon in October 2025 [1, 6]. For this event, I developed the core backend infrastructure and multi-vector RAG pipeline for CoCo AI, a clinical healthcare assistant powered by AWS Bedrock [5, 7]. Earlier that year, in April 2025, my team also won the RAISE-25 Hackathon hosted by Public Informatics at Rutgers [1, 6]. We placed in the top 3 out of 119 competing teams by engineering an advanced NLP and sentiment modeling system that successfully analyzed and processed over 10,000 articles, a complex project that we ultimately presented to industry experts [5].

During my professional tenure at Freshworks, my focus on product excellence and rapid innovation was recognized through multiple internal and global awards [1, 6, 8]. I am a three-time recipient of the prestigious "200 Not Out" award for Product Development, earning this distinction in Q3 2021, Q2 2022, and Q3 2022 for my sustained contributions to enterprise-scale features [1, 6, 8]. Additionally, at the very start of my career during my internship, I secured 3rd place overall in the 2020 Freshworks Global Hackathon [1, 6, 9, 10]. I achieved this by designing and prototyping an NLP-based HR chatbot using Dialogflow and Node.js that was projected to reduce support ticket volume by 40% [9, 10].

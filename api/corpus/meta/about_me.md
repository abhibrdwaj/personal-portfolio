# About Me

I am Abhinav Bharadwaj, a software engineer with about four years of experience spanning full-stack development, distributed backend systems, and applied AI. I hold a Master of Science in Computer Science from Rutgers University, where I also worked as a Graduate Teaching Assistant, both completed in December 2025. Since January 2026 I've been the founding backend engineer at Kidture Health, a pediatric health startup, where I own everything from schema design and 39 production migrations to AWS infrastructure, compliance, and the clinical AI pipelines built on the Anthropic Claude API. Before that, I spent just under four years at Freshworks in Chennai, India, where I worked my way up to Senior Software Engineer, building a RAG ingestion pipeline for the Freddy AI platform, scaling backend systems (Kafka, Sidekiq, PostgreSQL) to handle over a million daily background tasks, and leading a billing-engine overhaul for enterprise ITSM customers. My day-to-day stack is Python and FastAPI on the backend, PostgreSQL (including pgvector), Redis, and Docker for infrastructure, and I hold certifications in LLM Fundamentals from HuggingFace and AI Agents in LangGraph from DeepLearning.AI. I'm most interested in the intersection of backend systems engineering and applied AI, particularly where the two meet in healthcare: building things like retrieval pipelines, safety guardrails, and clinical NLP that hold up to real production constraints rather than staying at demo quality.

Communication is a strong part of how I work, not just the engineering itself. As a Graduate Teaching Assistant at Rutgers, I regularly broke down systems concepts like memory management and concurrency for students who were seeing them for the first time. As primary technical lead for the ESM-to-AI integration at Freshworks, I communicated scope and trade-offs across teams to hit a 30-day launch deadline, and I mentored three junior engineers on Rails, API design, and testing. I've also presented technical work to non-technical audiences directly, including presenting an NLP and sentiment-modeling project to industry experts after placing top 3 out of 119 teams at the RAISE-25 hackathon. Across all of this, I'm comfortable explaining technical trade-offs to both engineers and non-technical stakeholders, adjusting the level of detail to the audience.

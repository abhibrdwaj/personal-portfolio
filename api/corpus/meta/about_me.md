# About Me: AI & Software Engineering Profile

I am currently completing my Master of Science in Computer Science at Rutgers University while working as a Founding Engineer at a stealth healthcare startup in New York City [2-4]. I am passionate about solving complex problems at the intersection of software engineering and applied AI, particularly in multi-agent orchestration, healthcare tech, and AI interpretability [1].
